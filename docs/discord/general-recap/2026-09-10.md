# Community Recap — #general

**September 10, 2026** | 1:57 AM UTC – 11:54 PM UTC

## From Cassie

Cassie provided updates on the highly anticipated .25 release, noting she's "still working through a few items" including improved performance bounds on sync, frame requests, and materialization — which should help with archive timeouts and frame times. She also addressed a reported issue from otter regarding high CPU usage on large-core hosts (https://github.com/QuilibriumNetwork/monorepo/issues/648), which involves Rayon/Crossbeam overhead in recursive operations on a 256-logical-CPU server.

On the topic of moderation and free speech, Cassie stated that for Quorum spaces, "communities can choose how to self-police" and that the team "will never know what someone is saying in a space, nor do we ever want to." For the Farcaster integration, she explained the protocol is uncensored, but mobile clients are required by app store policy to have reporting/filtering. The plan is to take a Discord-style approach where users can log in on desktop and disable the filter if they choose.

Regarding the Farcaster bid, Cassie confirmed that Neynar will be the first to announce anything on that front. She also acknowledged a QNS issue raised by Trystan, saying "we will get it resolved."

## .25 Release Status

Community members asked about the status of .25 and how many items remain. Cassie responded that she'll share more "as soon as I get the build back to green (and testnet confirmed)." Mr.Quil expressed optimism that .25 represents a significant milestone toward release.

## Seniority Accrual Issue

stevesweerto reported a detailed technical issue: seniority stopped accruing on a healthy node that continues submitting reward proofs, spanning 5,523 frames with zero credit. They also flagged a separate issue where `build_vote_openings` produced nothing (no readable replicas for the shard), meaning frames carry no storage attestation. Cassie did not respond to this in the visible messages.

## Moderation Discussion

A debate emerged around banning/muting features on Quorum. Tyger explained that while the standard client respects DELETE messages, custom clients could be built to ignore deletions and maintain immutable local logs. Cassie's position was that free speech is a right, even for those saying "something wrong, dumb, hateful, etc." — defending the right of users to be wrong.

## EU Encryption News

Miggy shared a link about Europol lobbying the EU Commission to ban end-to-end encrypted communications. Pompey the Great provided context, noting the report is "nowhere near a policy recommendation or upcoming law" but rather a forward-looking document about privacy-enhancing technologies and law enforcement access. Community members discussed implications for Quorum and the Farcaster app store presence.
