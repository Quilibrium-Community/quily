# Community Recap — #general

**November 27, 2026** | 12:06 AM UTC – 3:13 PM UTC

## Cassie's ECC Attack Announcement Goes Viral

Cassie's X post about her "rhoboat" attack on elliptic curve cryptography (ECC) went viral, reaching roughly 250,000 views. She clarified that while it's speculation whether OpenAI has independently discovered the same attack, it is *not* speculation that she has developed a viable ECC attack. She noted that the Astra model's refusal to help optimize it was a "model-level" refusal rather than a standard Daybreak guard, which she found alarming because it implies the model understands the attack and how to build it.

Cassie explained the attack's evolution: historically it required "NSA Utah absurd scale datacenter and a year to break one key," but hardware requirements have dropped substantially in recent years. She estimated the current cost at roughly $500k/hour when amortizing data center costs, with billions in upfront investment — making it a nation-state-level capability. She emphasized the attack's most likely targets are surveillance apparatuses or financial system disruption, not individual crypto wallets.

## Bridge and Wallet Concerns

Community members expressed urgency about the wQUIL→QUIL bridge and wallet SDK, especially given the ECC concerns. Cassie confirmed she's "prepping the wallet sdk" and noted tooling exists in qclient. Regarding potential ECC breaks affecting bridged assets, she outlined a war-gamed contingency: if something breaks, they'd take state at the block prior and provide a route from that point. She acknowledged the bridge contract will eventually need upgrading, but said it's "not a problem to address today."

## Quilscan Agent v1.2.6

Quilscan announced an upgrade adding Telegram notifications for:
- High disk usage on the node configuration partition
- High system memory usage
- Upload/download speeds above threshold (10-minute average)
- Unexpected node stops/restarts
- New Agent/Node versions and successful auto-updates
