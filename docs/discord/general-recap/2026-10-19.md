# Community Recap — #general

**October 19, 2026** | 1:26 AM UTC – 11:23 AM UTC

## From Cassie

Cassie released version .25 and updated the archives. She also updated the Coppice spec with a new research repo: [spec](https://github.com/QuilibriumNetwork/monorepo/discussions/580) and [repo](https://github.com/QuilibriumNetwork/coppice-research), fixing a missing protobuf.

She addressed the current frame halt, explaining it was caused by a coordinated restart happening simultaneously with an archive restart, which made app shard frames unqueryable for the archives. She confirmed the halt has stopped and noted there's no shard split limit—the ultimate limit is the number of logical shards on the network, approximately 121 million.

Cassie also shared her vision for a streaming traffic relay that would masquerade onion routing data over endpoints carrying video traffic, noting that long streaming sessions broken into flat segment sizes are perfect cover for onion traffic. She discussed Tor's weaknesses: too few nodes, lack of a native value layer, and heavy monitoring by state actors (US and Germany). She emphasized that privacy-preserving solutions exist for crime-related concerns, citing QStorage for Image and Video, and stated she'll never align with using crime as an excuse to remove privacy.

## Network Issues & Debugging

Black Swan reported two stuck app shards (`…00092700` and `…000c1d60`) where members reject every proposal except their own. She requested node operators with specific peer IDs to export logs using qclient-2.1.0.25 and DM them to her. She identified the root cause: members bootstrapping at a shard's tip replay only the anchor, missing accumulator records for the 8 frames below, causing nullification of every proposal.

Cassie found the slow execution issue was related to the prior rollout problem—a sweeping rollout happening at the same time as the branch update.

## PRs & Improvements

Black Swan opened [PR #786](https://github.com/QuilibriumNetwork/monorepo/pull/786) to recover missing window records at consensus start from certified headers. She also opened [issue #787](https://github.com/QuilibriumNetwork/monorepo/issues/787) about eviction risks—90 of 93 provers would be kicked if evictions were enabled. Cassie preferred per-allocation kicks, and Black Swan submitted [PR #788](https://github.com/QuilibriumNetwork/monorepo/pull/788) implementing that direction (evictions stay off by default).

## Dashboard Performance

Black Swan reported a memory leak in dashboard.quilibrium.com—a single Firefox tab using ~15.5 GB. Cassie suspected it's because they're pulling full frame content and frames are now huge. Black Swan noted the explorer refetches 10 endpoints every 30 seconds, with the largest being /provers/eviction-risk at 4.0 MB JSON, and suggested dropping the repeated public key to cut it to ~0.75 MB.

## Community Discussion

Jor raised concerns about information fragmentation across Telegram, Discord, and Quorum, suggesting consolidation. Trystan noted Telegram has ~1000 messages daily and that some community members are in countries where Discord is blocked while Telegram works, so consolidation isn't straightforward.
