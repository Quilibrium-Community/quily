# Community Recap — #general

**November 24, 2026** | 12:11 AM UTC – 4:13 PM UTC

## Node Earnings and TUI Estimation Issue

Oumlaut reported a discrepancy between the TUI's theoretical daily reward estimate and actual earnings. Black Swan investigated, initially suspecting an incomplete world state view. Oumlaut confirmed the World State was 75.6 GB (binary units), matching the full 81.2 GB network, ruling out that theory. Black Swan noted the gap likely lies in how the theory column converts reward per frame into reward per day, and said the team is tracing the issue and will post a fix. Later, Oumlaut reported the estimation had normalized to 1.2/1.0/1.2 Q/d.

Black Swan also noticed Oumlaut's qclient version appeared outdated, but Oumlaut clarified the binary was from quilscan, not the official qclient.

## QNS Points and Rewards

Quilbill asked Cassie to explain how QNS Points and upcoming rewards/governance rights are meant to work. Cassie said she would address it, and noted the next update to archives/explorer will yield data on total QUIL released daily, which will be displayable on the explorer. Otter observed that very little QUIL is being released each day now.

## PoMW Scaling

Monkey asked whether all nodes jointly divide the daily QUIL release or if each node takes a small portion. Cassie explained that PoMW scales according to logical shard division — earnings are reduced scaled by the number of logical shard groups. She noted this becomes less of an issue when new app shards are deployed.

## Quorum Beta Release Date

H asked for the date quorum beta was released. LaMat responded it was Christmas 2024, with a link to the announcement.

## Hex Key Pasting Issue

H reported an error when pasting a hex key on both the web app and native app. LaMat suggested posting in the appropriate channel. H later resolved it, noting the issue was that '1' was being changed to 'I' when pasting, possibly an Apple issue.
