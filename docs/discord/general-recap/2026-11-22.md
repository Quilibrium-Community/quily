# Community Recap — #general

**November 22, 2026** | 12:03 AM UTC – 1:11 PM UTC

## From Cassie

Cassie caught up after a long power outage and addressed several topics:

- **Dark pools**: Clarified that "dark pools" is a traditional finance term, not about money laundering — you still know who you're dealing with. She noted it was originally said as a joke that people took seriously. A DEX effectively enables dark pools depending on design, with the key difference being private vs. public CLOB. The bidirectional bridge coming back online will allow other coins to come in permissionlessly.

- **Node warnings**: The "rank buffer full" warnings are expected with more shards now. Version 0.26 will alleviate this via recursive proof creation, which chews away at the mempool rather than at the frame.

- **Lattice cryptography**: Cassie explained her choice of specific lattice constructions over NIST-approved ones, citing distrust of NIST and the imposed structure. She shared a new paper (https://nigelsmart.github.io/Sub-Exp-LWE/main.pdf) showing a sub-exponential attack on LWE — not a break, but a new crack in the armor. Falcon and NTRU Prime are unaffected. She reiterated preferring djb's takes over NIST consensus.

- **MetaVM and smart contracts**: Cassie clarified that Quilibrium has the compute primitive for multiparty applications, and MetaVM as an intrinsic will introduce EVM bytecode compatibility — enabling EVM apps, Solana apps, or anything on RISC-V to run on Q. She explained that MPC enables trustless atomic swaps via two-stage escrow with view/spend keys, and that MPCTLS could forego trusted oracles like Polymarket uses.

- **Version 2.1.1**: Marked by 10s frame times and recursive SNARK on global to cut down on proof size limits (i.e., 0.26).

- **QNS**: Will resolve to any address, could be a token. Governance on QNS points will roll out.

## Node Issues

Aza reported a wallet balance display issue — the official qclient counts only the 1,024 largest coins by default. Quilscan confirmed this and provided the fix: `qclient token balance --max-coins 2048`.

Lainexp shared node warnings about storage epoch proofs not being delivered and slow store reads. Cassie noted these are part of what 0.26 will address.
