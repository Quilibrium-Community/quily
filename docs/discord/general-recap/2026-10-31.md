**October 31, 2026** | 12:32 AM UTC – 11:19 AM UTC

## From Cassie

Cassie clarified that Klearu is a training and inference runtime — any content limitations would come from the model itself, not the runtime. She also shared a broadcast link: [Bold Horizons — Who Owns Social? The Farcaster Debate](https://x.com/i/broadcasts/1DxLdZBwrQPxm).

In a longer discussion on privacy and compliance, Cassie explained that while companies can be coerced into adding logging, they cannot be forced to *reduce* security. She cited Signal's sealed senders as an example of logs that are practically useless for linking messages to users. She also discussed the Apple/San Bernardino case, noting the FBI tried to coerce Apple into backdooring its software update mechanism, and Apple argued that speech cannot be compelled — only silence can. The US withdrew when Apple was willing to see it through. Cassie expressed sympathy for hard cases but disagreed with the desire to deanonymize people, as it has frequently been abused.

## Node Memory Issues and Fixes

TawaFiF reported memory problems on two regular nodes: swap usage that never returned to zero, high iowait, workers stuck in "consensus retry timer," and reward proofs dropping from ~2000/h to 3/h. Black Swan confirmed the pattern on their own node and traced two causes, both growing with seat count:

1. Each seat proposal loads and copies the entire storage replica (~0.9 GB) twice — a fix is in progress.
2. Each worker's database opens its own 256 MiB block cache.

Workaround: set `QUIL_ROCKSDB_BLOCK_CACHE_MB=64` in the node service environment. The 545s "CRDT commit lock wait" is caused by the retention pruner holding the lock, fixed by [PR #1064](https://github.com/QuilibriumNetwork/monorepo/pull/1064), waiting to be merged.

## Token Shielding and Dormant Identities

Black Swan explained that [PR #1056](https://github.com/QuilibriumNetwork/monorepo/pull/1056) (in the merge queue) adds `--submit-config` support to all token commands, including `token shield` and `token shield-all`. For dormant identities with pre-2.1 keystores, GSR asked if a config copy with only `p2p.peerPrivKey` swapped could shield directly into the running node's wallet. Black Swan confirmed this works, with caveats: use one copy per dormant identity, never start a node from the copy, and test on one identity first since it hasn't been run end-to-end.
