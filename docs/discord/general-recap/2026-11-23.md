# Community Recap — #general

**November 23, 2026** | 1:52 AM UTC – 4:18 PM UTC

## From Cassie

Cassie addressed the P=NP discussion, explaining that even if P=NP were proven, it wouldn't necessarily break cryptography — a polynomial-time solution could still be impractically slow, and the impact would depend entirely on *how* the proof works. She noted she's long believed P likely equals NP but in the "cool finding, basically useless" category. Separately, she stated her belief that ECC and RSA have limited time left regardless of the P=NP question.

On the Starkware community's reaction to a lattice problem break, Cassie called it premature FUD-spreading, noting STARKs are good (Quilibrium uses them too) but being all-in on them isn't a balance users would accept.

Regarding the NVIDIA/Jensen Huang situation, Cassie commented that the chip embargo is "dumb" and framed the overall game as "info asymmetry rather than compute asymmetry," calling export restrictions on GPUs a red herring.

On the wallet SDK, Cassie confirmed it's pending on the public megarpc release, which she's working to get live ASAP.

## Network & Node Issues

TawaFiF reported significant node issues after upgrading two nodes to v2.1.0.26: held seats mostly expiring instead of settling, archives accepting far fewer storage epoch proofs (11-64 vs 127-199 before), sends dropping ~3x, and daily credits falling from 2-4 QUIL to near zero. They asked whether archive pacing from issue #1438 (40s rest on "rank buffer full") could be holding proofs past their window, or if it's archive saturation from prover count doubling.

Oumlaut flagged a qclient showing theoretical emissions of 2319 Q/d for 9 workers with cert ratios over 90%. Cassie asked for the qclient version and shard addresses, assuming the theory calculation has an issue since emissions wouldn't issue that amount for 9 workers.

## Quilscan Update

Quilscan Agent 1.2.5 released, adding auto-updates for Releases Nodes. Users can enable it in Node Console → Settings, and the Agent will automatically install newer official Quilibrium releases. Dev Node builds publish ~30 minutes after updates land on the official branch.
