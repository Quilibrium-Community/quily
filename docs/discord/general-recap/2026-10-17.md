## Quorum Development and Access

Multiple users discussed Quorum, the community's messaging/social platform. Mr.Quil noted many updates had come through recently, including on Quorum. Finn highlighted that Quorum is "getting dev'd HARD" and praised a developer named Rustler for their work, suggesting they deserve community tokens. Trystan had trouble accessing the Quorum channel—the web version wasn't working, and they couldn't see any messages or forums. David provided guidance: copy the invite link, go to the Spaces section in the Quorum app, click the plus sign in the top right, and paste the invite link there. Kovuh asked whether Quorum is meant to be a mix of Discord and Twitter, with David clarifying the "Twitter" aspect was Farcaster. David also noted that messaging and spaces in Quorum are private, while the social aspect would have separation unless profiles are merged, though he wasn't sure how this would work given the hard fork.
