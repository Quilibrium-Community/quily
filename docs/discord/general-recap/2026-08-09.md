# Community Recap — #general

**August 9, 2026** | 3:43 AM UTC – 10:55 PM UTC

## Migration and v0.25 Progress

Cassie [LEAD DEV] provided multiple updates throughout the day on the migration process. The data format migration completed, and archive operators began moving to new hardware. By late evening, all five archive nodes were back online with caches set up, and consensus had begun. The catch-up was described as "smooth" with the fifth node catching up "in like ten seconds."

## v0.25 Release Timeline

When asked about the release timeline, Cassie explained there would be a short round of testing after migration, then the build would be published barring any odd reports. A small update to the .25 branch is needed to point to the updated archives. The release is waiting for approximately 2/3 of a terabyte of data to copy across four machines.

## Bridge After v0.25

Cassie indicated the bridge would likely come "pretty quickly after" v0.25 release, probably with a day in between for breathing room. She noted that when the bridge was previously up, people immediately tried to exploit it, so approaching it fresh-eyed is safer.

## Token/Collectible Unlock

VELVIL VR asked about the token/collectible unlock timeline. Cassie confirmed these are "not mutually exclusive and can happen in parallel" with other developments.

## Archive and Explorer Status

By 9:37 PM UTC, archives and the explorer were starting back up. Cassie noted the first run after migration is slower than normal due to cache setup. By 10:22 PM UTC, everything was back online for normal operations.

## Current Status

As of late evening, Cassie confirmed waiting for the first epoch to pass and confirm joins, then the second epoch for workers to activate. Frame times were reported at approximately 10 seconds.
