# Community Recap — #general

**November 8, 2026** | 1:55 AM UTC – 12:20 PM UTC

## Network Health Update

Black Swan reported a network issue affecting archives: they are refusing many node submissions due to "rank buffer full" errors, causing storage epoch proofs and coverage messages to fail delivery. The cause is that since the storage warmup began, nodes resend sector capacity messages multiple times per frame, filling archive buffers.

The fix is in [PR #1329](https://github.com/QuilibriumNetwork/monorepo/pull/1329) — sending capacity messages once per new frame instead of repeatedly. It awaits merge and release. No operator action is needed now; nodes retry on their own.

## Frame Interval and Shard Splitting

Mr.Franc asked whether the increased frame interval time is a concern and whether shard splitting is the cause. Black Swan clarified that the frame interval increase is a side effect of recent security-strengthening changes and the incident they led to. The team is evaluating optimizations for both regular and archive nodes.
