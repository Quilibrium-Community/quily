## Infrastructure & SSD Wear Optimization

Black Swan shared a detailed audit of build and test machine hardware impact:

- Build and test machines were writing about 13 TB per day to SSDs, roughly 7x what drives can sustain over three years. About 72% came from relinking test binaries full of debug info, mostly RocksDB's.
- Changes made: RocksDB, zstd, and lz4 now build without debug info in dev/test (reducing test relink writes by 35%, from 3.6 to 2.4 GB, and shrinking librocksdb.a from 1.15 GB to 35 MB). Proposed upstream: https://github.com/QuilibriumNetwork/monorepo/pull/1273
- Build caches are now handed between branches, test checks are batched into one build, heavy rebuilds go to drives with more wear headroom, and daily TRIM plus a watcher flag drives writing over budget.
- Next step: compressing debug sections at link time.
- Black Swan clarified these figures were for development SSD use cases, not regular node operation, but the goal is to also relieve regular node SSDs.
