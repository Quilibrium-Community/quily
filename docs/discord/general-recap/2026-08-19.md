# Quilibrium Discord Recap – 2026-08-19

## From Cassie
Cassie shared two major updates:

1. **Farcaster Acquisition**: Quilibrium is pursuing an acquisition of Farcaster. If accepted, the Farcaster app would become "quorum-ified" – moving Cloudflare services to qStorage and Privy to qKMS. Quorum's development plans remain unchanged. More details are not yet public.

2. **Node Work**: She is wiring up an archive to get better metrics on the 10-second frame issue. She found one reason archives were being hammered and will push a fix to significantly stabilize frame uptake. She is also addressing issues #620, #587, and #609.

When asked about timing, Cassie acknowledged it's tricky but called it a rare opportunity with mutual benefits for growth, consolidation, and app store approval (Farcaster already cleared major hurdles).

## Farcaster Acquisition Discussion
Community members reacted positively overall, with some questioning timing. Monkey asked whether mainnet stability should remain the priority. Cassie responded that the opportunity is rare and the mutual benefit outweighs timing concerns. Others noted it wouldn't impede core development and could be a real positive. Cassie added that Farcaster has already been in a Super Bowl ad, and it's better than letting a private equity firm "scoop them up."

## Node Technical Issues
TawaFiF reported detailed findings from running the amended #620 fix on a non-archive node. Backfill recovered ~29k frame records, but 5 chunks reported as "unservable" despite the heights being canonical and publicly retrievable on quilscan. The warning message incorrectly suggests the frames are uncommitted/orphaned when it's actually an archive-side gap. Cassie's planned archive fix may overlap with this.

Mr.Franc noted the 10-second-per-frame issue is back, likely being worked on.

## Cluster Mode Issue
stevesweerto reported a problem with remote data workers in cluster mode (v2.1.0.25). The worker's prover tree never converges, looping on "post-sync phase root still differs." Neither `enableMasterProxy: true` nor `false` resolves it. The worker ends up with 0 shard engines and 0 global frames. He asked for guidance on how remote workers should converge in this version.
