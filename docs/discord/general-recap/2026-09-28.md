# Community Recap — #general

**September 28, 2026** | 2:50 AM UTC – 12:21 PM UTC

## From Cassie

Cassie addressed concerns about Ethereum potentially copying Quilibrium's roadmap, noting that her latest blog post (["The Future's Present Past"](https://cassieheart.substack.com/p/the-futures-present-past)) mirrors what appears in Vitalik's recent writing. She argued ETH carries too much technical and state debt to catch up, particularly around ECC authorization data volume, and noted they haven't even built their RISC-V machine yet while Quilibrium already has the RISCV64IMAC instruction set.

On mainnet progress, Cassie reported 71 separate builds and test runs over the past three days. Mainnet data is now entering tests, with a dry run pass directly on archives completed. She noted it "mostly worked" but needs a few upgrade-related fixes on first boot to avoid a halt around updated consensus, then it's branch update time. She gave a go/no-go decision window of roughly 12 hours.

Cassie also clarified that QNS does not use a smart contract in the Ethereum sense—it's an application running as an alt-fee basis shard. She defined the four requirements for declaring mainnet live: rewards flowing, no halting/restart issues, handling prover churn, and new application deployments not interrupting the network.

## Ethereum Competition Discussion

A lengthy debate centered on whether Ethereum's recent announcements threaten Quilibrium's future. CarlXB argued ETH's ecosystem strength and marketing of similar concepts could erode Quilibrium's competitive advantage, especially since developer/user habits are hard to break once formed. Others countered that ETH's implementation timeline spans years, giving Quilibrium a substantial lead. Cassie noted ETH has delayed multiple upgrades (Pectra, Fusaka, Glamsterdam) and recently laid off developers.

## AI and Quilibrium's Future

Community members discussed whether AI could make Quilibrium obsolete. Mr.Quil argued AI needs the internet and will be integrated rather than replace it, while yogurt noted centralized AI companies lack incentives to build decentralized solutions. CarlXB suggested AI could help accelerate development but worried it might also help competitors catch up faster.

## QNS Minting Issue

Quilbill raised a previously reported QNS mint contract issue where funds were absorbed without triggering a NameRegistered event, providing a transaction ID. Cassie asked for clarification and directed product feedback to DMs.
