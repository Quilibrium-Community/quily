# Community Recap — #general

**October 16, 2026** | 12:02 AM UTC – 10:07 AM UTC

## From Cassie

Cassie confirmed that the Farcaster situation has driven a large influx of users to Quorum/Hypersnap, with roughly two-thirds of the remaining original purple app users migrating over. She noted that people are already digging into Quorum's codebase to contribute, including someone building a webapp integrating Hypersnap. She described this as "an important part of strategy" and a positive development despite the chaos.

On the technical side, Cassie released an update to node version .25 that addresses an issue where app shard quorums were stalling. The previous fix aimed to avoid skipped cert rounds but caused all shards to halt because some provers were inactive. The new update should resolve this, though it won't take effect until the next epoch.

Regarding network traffic, Cassie clarified that the reported 80% reduction in traffic was likely due to large committee dropoff rather than the update itself, and traffic has since recovered. She noted that bandwidth will be a key competitive factor for miners, as the network's primary utility requires bandwidth.

## Node Issues and Worker Behavior

Guru_Meditation reported a field observation from two nodes running 2.1.0.25: both synced and following frames, but one worker on each node was stuck in an archive-recovery retry loop with "app shard frame missing BLS signature" errors. The same shard filter failed against multiple archive peers, suggesting the archived frames themselves lack the BLS signature. Cassie acknowledged the issue was found and being addressed.

TawaFiF provided detailed technical feedback on ProverConfirm preparation becoming very slow after a recent build, with confirmations taking 157-279 minutes and missing epoch boundaries. They noted a potential cause in the storage replica leaf selection changes from PR #733, and that an in-progress attempt blocks other attempts for those filters.

## Farcaster/Hypersnap Migration

Community members discussed the fallout from the Farcaster situation, with Cassie noting she was banned for her posts. The Hypersnap Telegram group saw a surge in activity, with users expressing interest in the fork. Cassie mentioned that Quorum already posts to Hypersnap while still supporting some Farcaster API calls. Community members suggested names for the fork including Farside, Castora, Upcaster, Speekr, and Castr.

## Mainnet and Network Status

Finn asked about the current warning/halt status on the network, with Cassie explaining the shard quorum fix situation. LUUK asked whether mainnet or GTA would come first, and Cassie remained confident mainnet would win. Otter shared a wQUIL holder analytics dashboard link for tracking token distribution and large-holder behavior.
