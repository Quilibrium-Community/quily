# Community Recap — #general

**July 31, 2026** | 1:51 AM UTC – 8:08 AM UTC

## Quilibrium Daily Recap – 2026-07-31

### From Cassie
Cassie offered her perspective on crypto regulation: Trump was a "grey effect" — he removed problematic people, but Congress has failed to deliver regulatory clarity, so the legal posture remains fragile. She maintains a conservative approach for Quilibrium because of that.

**Status update:** A v0.25 update is being pushed shortly, and the Quorum mobile app also received an update. The two big fixes in .25 relate to cluster mode workers and epoch boundary edge cases. Cassie described .25 as "extremely close" to full operation. Releases are also being moved to qstorage.

### Quorum Mobile Update
Quorum mobile 2.1.0-47 is available on TestFlight and Google Play, with a sideload link provided: https://releases.quilibrium.com/qm-2.1.0-47.apk. Cassie confirmed that TestFlight will continue to receive beta releases alongside the App Store, so users can get updates faster.

LaMat noted that some app bar buttons (e.g., QNS) are disabled but shown intentionally rather than hidden. The update includes UI improvements, client-side fixes for message transport, and new **delivery/read receipts** for DMs (optional, toggleable in profile settings). A known issue: some users report notifications triggering without actual new messages. LaMat directed users to the bugs channel: <#1456158029627129961>.

Main pain points for testing remain sync (profile/space changes across devices) and message transport, especially on mobile DMs — desktop-to-desktop works much better.
