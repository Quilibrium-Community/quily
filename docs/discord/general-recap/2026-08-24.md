# Community Recap — #general

**August 24, 2026** | 1:11 AM UTC – 6:23 AM UTC

## From Cassie

Lead developer Cassie provided several important updates:

- **Farcaster acquisition clarification**: The proposal is about bringing the Farcaster protocol under community stewardship (Quorum), while the Farcaster app, domains, etc. would need a corporate owner. This would effectively consolidate Quorum and the Farcaster app, bringing privacy preservation, end-to-end encrypted chats, and group chats to Farcaster users broadly.

- **Node update 0.25**: Released, resolving the splitting issue. Archives are performing a consolidation step, so frames are not moving yet.

- **Quorum development progress**:
  - A bounty for fixing feed rendering has received two submissions, one accepted.
  - The interactive post feature (snaps/frames v3) emulator has been updated to reflect the current state of the snap compute proposal, with examples available on GitHub: [github.com/farcasterorg/snap/tree/fip-21-snap-compute](https://github.com/farcasterorg/snap/tree/fip-21-snap-compute) (including a demo of sprite functionality and compute suspend/resume behavior).
  - Discussions have reopened with a wallet transaction simulation security services provider to explore privacy-preserving integration.
  - Another Farcaster client team has signed on to the hypersnap plan, regardless of acquisition outcome.

## Farcaster Community Stewardship Discussion

Aoki clarified that the proposal is not Q acquiring Farcaster in the traditional sense but rather placing the protocol under community stewardship. Cassie confirmed it’s “kind of both” – the protocol goes to community stewardship, while the app and domains would need corporate ownership. The consolidation would extend Quorum’s privacy features to Farcaster users.

## Node Update and Archives Maintenance

Cassie released node update 0.25, which resolves the splitting issue. Archives are currently undergoing a consolidation step, causing frames to not be moving yet. Blackslne reported connection issues, and Cassie confirmed archives are still under maintenance.

## Quorum Development Updates

Beyond Cassie’s contributions, the feed rendering bounty is progressing, the snap compute emulator is updated, and new client team adoption of hypersnap continues. Aza asked about hiring and training developers to maintain the codebase; Cassie confirmed plans to do so in due time.
