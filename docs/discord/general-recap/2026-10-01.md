# Community Recap — #general

**October 1, 2026** | 1:16 AM UTC – 11:34 AM UTC

## Node Status and Rewards

Community members discussed node performance and reward distribution following recent updates. Several users reported their nodes showing "joining" status for extended periods. Black Swan explained the epoch process: a node announces joining (epoch 1), confirms joining (epoch 2), and becomes active (epoch 3), with each epoch lasting 720 frames. Some nodes were observed with 100+ active shards, which Mr.Quil recalled may relate to multi-shard proofs Cassie mentioned previously.

## Technical Issues and Fixes

Black Swan reported that his node stopped receiving frames due to temporary disk space overflow. A restart was required to resume normal operation, as clearing disk space alone wasn't sufficient. He noted this is a security/integrity feature that prevents database connection when disk space is low, and he's exploring ways to automate recovery while maintaining the security benefits.

Black Swan also mentioned an "archive intervention" occurring, suggesting Cassie is actively fixing something—possibly related to issue #664 on the monorepo. He speculated the maintenance could address app shard prover committee/certificate divergences and/or shard split/merge issues.

## PRs and Development

Black Swan shared that he's completing a review of his open pull requests, noting that many issues he reported were fixed more professionally in the main codebase than in his contributions. He opened several new PRs addressing freshly identified minor deviations. He also filed issue #665 regarding non-trivial impact and trade-offs for cluster nodes.
