## From Cassie

Cassie announced a new proposal in the GitHub discussions section: **Coppice: Decentralized Asynchronous PQ E2EE Group Messaging** ([GitHub Discussion #580](https://github.com/QuilibriumNetwork/monorepo/discussions/580)). This addresses the need for a post-quantum (PQ) group messaging protocol, as the existing Triple Ratchet (TR) uses elliptic curve crypto which isn't PQ-secure.

She explained that after investigating PQ alternatives to TR, no algebraic construction preserved TR's structure without being "outrageously expensive in bandwidth and compute." However, lattice cryptography offers different capabilities that, with structural changes, can work just as well. She claims this outperforms other PQ solutions like Post-Quantum MLS (e.g., X-Wing+X25519).

When asked why she's pursuing this now, Cassie stated there's a reason she changed her position on the PQ timeline, but "that is all I can say for now."
