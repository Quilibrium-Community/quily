# Community Recap — #general

**September 2, 2026** | 2:02 AM UTC – 8:44 AM UTC

## Network Halt Investigation and Resolution

**Cassie** [LEAD DEV] reported that the network halted at the epoch 1084 boundary. Initially suspected to be a split that the speedup from epoch 1083 should have handled, it turned out to be a consensus halt: all archives failed to materialize “780480.” Cassie confirmed the network did the right thing by stopping when unsafe, and began root cause analysis.

Later, the cause was identified as “something dumb.” Cassie pushed an update to the archives; the restart will fix the issue, and the update should prevent freezes on future epochs. After restarting, archives were still booting slowly but progressing.

**Mr.Quil** and others noted this is the furthest the network has ever gotten, and the correct behavior of the network was a positive sign. Cassie clarified the halt wasn’t a true stop—it was running the slowest possible path to determine shard sizes.

## From Cassie

- Investigated the halt at epoch 1084 boundary, traced to a consensus halt due to archive materialization failure.
- Provided a patch for archives to resolve the underlying condition and prevent future freezes.
