# Community Recap — #general

**September 16, 2026** | 1:02 AM UTC – 5:59 AM UTC

## From Cassie

Cassie provided a substantive update on the current test passes for the 2.0 branch. The bundle test for minting from earned tokens into an immediate spend — which creates a new hypergraph deploy of metadata, a new token application, and a mint out — has succeeded. Similar test passes have completed for mint with payment logic, and are currently being run for mint with proof. Passing those should allow the team to move forward on updating the branch.

In response to a question about shard-out completion, Cassie clarified that shard-out works without issue and has since the last update. The current focus is on resolving rewards and subsequent network interactions.

Cassie also addressed a question about why tests always seem to work perfectly but reality differs. She explained that testnet cannot accurately simulate mainnet without costing the same as running all nodes on mainnet. The alternative approach is to isolate the elements that make the two networks behave differently, get those behaving the same, and continue with tests.

## Development Progress

Finn asked for clarification on the current iteration goals — specifically whether the team is still working toward completing the shard-out with splitting and then rewards being minted. Cassie confirmed the current state: shard-out is complete, and the team is working through reward minting and network interactions.

## Community Discussion

A community member named yogurt shared an invite to an unofficial Telegram group for active Quilibrium participants. The group is capped at 100 members or until mainnet launches, whichever comes first. The stated purpose is to preserve the "curated vibes" of the current quiet development period, similar to OG groups in other crypto communities like ETH and Cardano. The group was at 65 members at the time of the message.

## Moderation Discussion

Poorest noted that Quilibrium's Discord is notably lenient compared to other crypto communities — pointing out that someone who called the project a scam would be instantly banned in most other discords but remains here. Poorest expressed that this leniency is fine but pushed back on complaints about speech suppression, noting the "leash is 1000km long" compared to other groups.
