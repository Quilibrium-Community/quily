# Community Recap — #general

**October 24, 2026** | 12:05 AM UTC – 9:55 AM UTC

## App Shard Progress

Black Swan provided a status update on the app shards: 96/105 are working (the dashboard's claim of 106 is a mistake, as it counts the global shard — this is fixed and pending deployment). The last 9 blocked shards point to defects being addressed. An archive/explorer and regular node update is being prepared to resolve discovered defects and improve app shard health KPIs. Later in the day, Black Swan reported 101/105 shards producing frames regularly.

## Archive Node Fix

Black Swan shared PR #928, an archive-side fix for empty-frame holes: archives now store and serve the body of a frame they executed from its carried header, and owe and fetch late bodies. Once deployed, they also fill an existing hole when a peer asks for it (of consecutive old holes, only the last). A companion PR for regular-node replay follows after review. Black Swan gave the go-ahead to proceed with archive/explorer node updates.

## Node Reachability and Diagnostics

Black Swan emphasized the importance of node operators having their nodes reachable with diagnostic data enabled, noting this feedback loop is a key contributor to rapid progress. Instructions were shared for setting up firewalls (on VDS) or port forwarding (at home), with a link to the docs on default ports to open. The Check Ports button in the Node Console was also mentioned.

## Roadmap Milestones

In response to a question about remaining hurdles, Black Swan clarified the milestones: 1) all app shards operational, 2) keeping them operational as nodes come and go, and 3) optimizing frame intervals. Work on frame intervals is already underway with Cassie.

## From Cassie

Cassie discussed WikiLeaks and Assange in the context of anonymous evidence reporting platforms. She noted that Assange handled the ingestion of tips quite well, but the problem is when a powerful nation state wants you dead. She reiterated her mantra: "metadata kills people," and said anything that can ensure people stay alive where no other option is available is a win. She called the charges against Assange absurd and the extradition effort an insane bending of international law. She affirmed that anonymous evidence reporting is an incredibly important use case for Quilibrium.
