# Community Recap — #general

## From Cassie

Cassie addressed the *Silicon Valley* comparison directly, noting that the show's story began with a compression algorithm, while Quilibrium's shards are the opposite—they require *more* storage to create proofs of replication. She called the show "an incredible satire of how fucked silicon valley actually is" and confirmed this isn't the first time the comparison has come up.

She also mentioned being "full tilt today on all fronts," which is why she hasn't been around much, and acknowledged receiving node performance data from a community member, saying she'd take a look.
