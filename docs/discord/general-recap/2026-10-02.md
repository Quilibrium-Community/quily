# Community Recap — #general

**October 2, 2026** | 12:43 AM UTC – 10:49 AM UTC

## Dashboard Sync Issues
sup reported that the node dashboard is displaying incorrect information—showing nodes with hundreds of active shards when only 24 exist, and claiming every node faces immediate eviction for the past 24 hours. Cassie acknowledged the report and said she is looking into it, noting she has been collecting and reviewing metrics from archives to address previously identified issues.

## Mainnet Timeline Optimism
Several community members expressed confidence that mainnet could launch this year. Mr.Quil stated he's been following the project closely and believes the team has never been closer to mainnet. yogurt relayed that according to Cassie, what remains before mainnet "shouldn't take weeks" and could potentially happen by end of month, while cautioning that reality may differ. Black Swan identified the next blockers as GitHub issue #664 and shard split/merge functionality, suggesting both being solved "should lead to an operational mainnet."

## From Cassie
Cassie addressed a question about quantum computing (QC) risk for new seed wallets. She explained that if a wallet has never signed anything, it's safe because only the address (a hash) appears on-chain—the public key remains unknown and thus not vulnerable to QC attacks.

She also clarified her energy drink preference (Bang, not Monster), which sparked a brief community exchange. Cassie confirmed she is actively investigating the dashboard sync issues reported by sup.

## Node Performance and Shard Joining
Otter reported their worker unable to join the shard ring all day. Black Swan shared their own allocations (13 Active, 2 Joining) and noted major progress after the recent v2.1.0.25 push following the pause. Black Swan suggested using an LLM assistant to check logs for troubleshooting. A new green CI prerequisite was shared via GitHub PR #667.

## Community Contributions Discussion
LUUK suggested the community contribute funds so Black Swan could work on the project full-time. Black Swan graciously declined, stating that lack of financial compensation isn't limiting his contributions, and he prefers to combine technical contributions with his day job to support his family. He expressed admiration for Cassie, the Treasury members, signers, and moderators working through challenging times.

## wQUIL Transaction Display
A discussion arose about wQUIL transactions on DexScreener appearing incorrect. Community members explained that the display only shows the portion of transactions going through the Uniswap V3 pool, while some transactions route through multiple pools simultaneously.
