## Farcaster Bid & Fork Contingency

Cassie discussed the ongoing Farcaster/Neynar bid process, revealing there are three parties in the bidding: one unknown entity, Mask Network, and Q. She noted that while community sentiment is "pretty overwhelming," Neynar ultimately makes the call. Cassie also shared two timestamped Farcaster casts — one from 20 days before the Neynar announcement and another from Rish — hinting at possible connections.
