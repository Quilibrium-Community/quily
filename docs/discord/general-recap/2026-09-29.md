# Community Recap — #general

**September 29, 2026** | 12:00 AM UTC – 11:41 AM UTC

## From Cassie

Cassie addressed a question about Near Intents and whether it compares to Klearu. She clarified that Near Intents provides privacy only via TEEs (Trusted Execution Environments), which she described as "a painfully failed premise that amounts to 'just trust me, bro.'" She elaborated on why TEEs are fundamentally flawed: hardware-based enclaves can be compromised through side-channel attacks like rowhammer, and the modern threat landscape—with millions of spawnable agents capable of bespoke attacks—makes the "high-end state actor" defense obsolete. She noted the irony that crypto, which began with Bitcoin removing trusted intermediaries, has come full circle to solutions relying on trusted hardware signing. She also mentioned she was doing a thorough review of archives to ensure everything is properly documented.

## QNS Domains and Website Hosting

1bxyz asked whether QNS names will function as website domains, replacing traditional registrars like GoDaddy. Community members confirmed that QNS names will use the `.q` top-level domain, purchased once for life with no yearly renewals. However, they noted that `.q` domains cannot be resolved natively in standard browsers yet—browser extensions or proxies are needed, similar to how `.onion` works on Tor. Native browser support is on the roadmap. Black Swan added that domain leasing/subdomain rental tooling isn't available yet, and without it, lease agreements would rely on trust rather than enforced binding—something the project aims to minimize. Dienosaur noted that more QNS details will come once focus returns to it after mainnet.
