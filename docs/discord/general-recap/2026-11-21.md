# Community Recap — #general

**November 21, 2026** | 12:36 AM UTC – 1:29 PM UTC

## From Cassie

Cassie hosted a live stream on Twitch and X (Twitter), covering Quilibrium's progress. She noted the project's tech spans multiple verticals that could each be 8-9 figure companies on their own. She also announced that `.26` subpatch 0 will be on dev builds soon, and that it will include relational database functionality, which will be open sourced.

Cassie also helped a user with a QNS registration issue via direct message, and joked about scambots being triggered by bridge discussions.

## Bridge Discussion

Community members discussed the upcoming bridge extensively. Key points from the stream recap:

- The bridge will be bidirectional, supporting all ERC-20 tokens and ETH itself, not just Q/wQUIL as originally planned
- Future plans include bridging BTC and other assets
- Assets bridged over will be post-quantum (PQ) shielded, enabling darkpools
- Cassie declined to give an ETA due to past issues with ETAs, which led to community jokes about "edging" and "holding the line"

## Quantum Risk Discussion

Aza raised concerns about holding assets on EVM chains due to quantum computing risks. The discussion noted:

- Ethereum is not yet post-quantum
- The day current elliptic curve crypto is broken is called "Q Day" and could be 2 years away
- Users might need to bridge to Q or similar post-quantum safe havens if Ethereum isn't ready in time

## Node Earnings Question

VELVIL VR asked about nodes not earning for about a week. Cassie indicated the `.26` subpatch would address this, with dev builds coming soon.

## SQL on Quilibrium

ramgos asked if anyone had created an SQL service on top of Quilibrium. Cassie clarified that the relational functionality in `.26` subpatch 0 would serve this purpose.

## Stream Logistics

The stream was briefly delayed due to Streamlabs issues but started shortly after. Community members shared links to watch on Twitch and X. Several users asked about a recording link, and some were still watching later in the day.
