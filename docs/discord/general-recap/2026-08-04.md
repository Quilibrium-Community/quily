## Quorum Infrastructure Clarified by Cassie

A discussion emerged around what infrastructure actually secures Quorum. Cassie [LEAD DEV] clarified that prover nodes are involved in **routing and securing** Quorum messages, but not in **storing** them (messages are transient) or **validating through consensus**. She explained that Quorum uses the network's secure encrypted channels, oblivious query structure for wallet RPC, and onion routing for voice/video calls. She also noted that prover nodes do not earn rewards for carrying Quorum traffic, as those messages are indistinguishable from normal network traffic.
