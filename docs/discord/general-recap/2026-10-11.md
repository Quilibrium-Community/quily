# Community Recap — #general

**October 11, 2026** | 2:44 AM UTC – 11:33 AM UTC

## Node Sync Issues and Allocations

Several node operators reported issues this morning with their allocations showing as "Leaving" and "Blocked" simultaneously. Mr.Quil provided a detailed technical breakdown of the problem, identifying four root causes:

1. Missing historical data — no archive served outgoing records and no peer served historical frame 878
2. Missing BLS signature on app shard frames (post-genesis frames must be signed)
3. Shard falling behind — frame gaps during synchronization
4. Consensus unable to start — app CW committee build failed and predecessor state not recovered through its seal

Mr.Quil noted this appears related to PR #719 on GitHub, which addresses the archive side of the issue. Steve confirmed the analysis was accurate, explaining that the "Leaving" status is a shard reshuffle rather than a fault, and that nodes cleared themselves automatically at the epoch boundary yesterday without any client-side intervention. Black Swan had previously advised patience, noting Cassie is working on the real fix.

## From Cassie
