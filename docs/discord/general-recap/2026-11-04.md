# Community Recap — #general

**November 4, 2026** | 1:38 AM UTC – 1:13 PM UTC

## From Cassie
- Cassie noted that Devcon attendees are organizing around "the fork," while those not at Devcon are preparing a "message" — she declined to elaborate further.
- She shared that the major development hurdles are behind the project, with mostly small fixes remaining before mainnet.
- Cassie mentioned the GitHub merge queue was slow, linking to the v2.1.0.25 queue page.
- She highlighted the potential of LoRa radio adapters for Q miners, noting they could keep nodes operational even when internet connectivity fails.

## Network & Development Progress
- Black Swan reported a "final merge sprint" toward version .114, which will include 80+ fixes. He emphasized proactive monitoring to catch issues before they affect nodes.
- GitHub caused several delays: SSH failures (~70% of the time), CI/merge runs failing due to disk space, and inconsistent Ubuntu versions across VMs. The team pinned Ubuntu versions and tuned CI to work around these.
- Black Swan clarified that .114 could be the final test version before official release, but he's currently focused on core Q functionality in the monorepo.

## Rewards & Credit Logic
- TawaFiF reported measured rewards at ~45% of qclient's 24h estimate on their nodes. Black Swan explained the gap: each shard frame pays only the quorum (~70%) of members whose finalize votes formed its certificate, while the estimate assumes payment every frame.
- Black Swan confirmed the estimate is being fixed to count the quorum share. He also noted the credit accrual logic is being hardened to align with the project's vision, with node/qclient estimates being fine-tuned so actual rewards should meet theoretical ones.

## Node Operations
- A user asked about moving workers from rings 1 and 2 to ring 0. Black Swan advised keeping the node up — workers get promoted as other provers leave lower rings.
- Mainnet health partially degraded due to a data center outage causing loss of many provers. The team is investigating ways to make the network more resilient to such events. Cassie asked if it was the 9 out of 109 shards affected.

## Community Sentiment
- Several members expressed excitement about mainnet and deploying apps. Mr.Quil noted the community's satisfaction has shifted — no more complaints, just confidence that release is near. Black Swan responded, "I prefer actions to speak, rather than words, but some people also need words."
