# Community Recap — #general

**October 12, 2026** | 12:31 AM UTC – 1:52 PM UTC

## From Cassie

Cassie shared insights on the timeline for Quilibrium's quantum computing (QC) capabilities, noting that QC is projected for 2028-2030, but an ECC break could come sooner. She linked to Scott Aaronson's blog post "The Mathocalypse" as a resource for understanding why some researchers feel this way about advances in discrete logarithm problem (DLP) solvers. Cassie also mentioned that the "rhoboat attack got faster," referring to progress on cryptographic attacks.

Regarding the current network status, Cassie confirmed that getting archives back to 10-second intervals is a key part of the current build. She also addressed the Farcaster discussion, noting that the story is "far from over" and that "some surprising names have entered the ring in working together to see DeSo happen."

## Network Status & Frames

The community discussed the ongoing frame halts on the network. Steve advised against updating nodes mid-halt, recommending patience over restarts: "I only update once the frames are advancing steadily again." Mr.Quil shared that he lets rewards flow in without restarting. Later, Aza reported "Frames are framing again," though Mr.Franc noted that not all shards are earning. lewsid2.0 estimated an 80% chance of mainnet launch this year, while Mr.Franc was less optimistic.

## Node Migration & Token Keys

CarlXB asked whether token key files mined in November 2024 can run on the new version. Tyger confirmed they can, explaining that the keys.yml file and the p2p.peerPrivKey and key.keyManagerFile.encryptionKey from the config are needed. CarlXB also mentioned holding mainnet tokens not yet bridged to ETH, waiting for confirmation they won't be lost before proceeding.

## Privacy & Decentralization Discussion

Pompey the Great raised the Yandex datacenter attacks as an example of why decentralized solutions are superior to centralized ones, noting Russia's struggles with internet and banking due to lack of distributed architecture. This sparked discussion about decentralization on a global scale, with yogurt commenting on the irony of supporting decentralized internet but not decentralized world powers.

## Meme Coin & NFT Talk

Community members discussed the potential for the first meme coin on Quilibrium, with Miggy suggesting it should simply be called "meme" as the first coin printed in QUIL after the native token. yogurt noted that Quilibrium's privacy features could usher in an NFT PFP resurgence, comparing potential collections to Ethereum's CryptoPunks or Bitcoin's NodeMonkes.

## Farcaster Ecosystem Debate

CarlXB argued that Farcaster's existing web3 ecosystem is crucial for Quilibrium's development, citing the need for influence within established communities. Cassie clarified that the Farcaster joke originated with Mike of Rainbow Wallet and that the story continues to develop.
