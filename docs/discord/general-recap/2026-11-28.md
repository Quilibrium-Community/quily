# Community Recap — #general

**November 28, 2026** | 4:28 AM UTC – 2:16 PM UTC

## From Cassie
Cassie dismissed a new paper as "effectively meaningless" because it requires storing 2^129 points, which is impractical. She also commented on Google's new multi-party compute (MPC) custody service for Bitcoin, calling it "google discovers MPC" and noting it "literally does nothing to 'save' bitcoin." She shared a tweet highlighting that Quilibrium offers decentralized MPC for self-custody, contrasting it with Google's centralized approach.

## Google MPC News
Miggy shared news that Google is building a crypto custody service for banks and institutions using multi-party compute, splitting private keys across separate systems. Cassie noted this is just centralized MPC and does nothing to help Bitcoin. sickojacko pointed out the service is for banks and institutions, not Bitcoin users.

## Bridge Status
Nikos148 asked about the bridge timeline, expressing concern about staying on ETH. mensano said no date but "soon enough." CarlXB noted the need to bridge tokens back to mainnet. Cassie confirmed she's "working as quickly as possible to get megarpc up" to enable that.

## Bridge Risk Discussion
Mental asked whether bridging ETH locks funds in a smart contract on Ethereum, exposing users to smart contract risk. LaMat explained that after bridging wquil to quil, the wquil is gone, so there's no remaining risk on the ETH side. Mental then asked about ECC break scenarios for locked ETH/USDC. LaMat said if a token's value depends on collateral on ETH, losing that collateral means losing the bridged asset's value. Cassie clarified that USDC is more complicated due to Circle's control over the contract, which could get messy in an ECC break. For ETH, the bridge contract relies on non-ECC cryptography, so risk lives solely in the contract code, not ECC breakage concerns.

## QVault Password Manager
virago shared an experimental password manager browser extension built on QStorage and QKMS. The vault key is protected by a QKMS key split between the hosted service and the browser, with encrypted backups in QStorage, passkey unlock, and cross-computer sync. It's test-data only for now. Link: https://github.com/qcenter/qvault
