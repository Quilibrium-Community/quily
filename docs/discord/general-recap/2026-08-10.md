# Community Recap — #general

**August 10, 2026** | 1:28 AM UTC – 11:55 PM UTC

## Network Progress and Frame Stability

Multiple community members reported that frame intervals appear stable at 10 seconds, with consistent frame progression. Cassie confirmed this is working as expected, noting: "if your own node is reporting close to 677554 it's keeping up." She explained that after a coordinated restart of the archives around frame 677000, the network has been reliably upholding the 10-second frame time.

Active workers are not yet showing on dashboards, but Cassie clarified the timeline: the previous epoch processes joins, the current epoch confirms them, and active status will appear in the next epoch (~113 frames away, about 19 minutes at 10s per frame).

> **Note:** One epoch is **720 frames** on mainnet (`epoch = floor(frame / 720)`), roughly 2 hours at a 10-second frame time. The "~113 frames" above is the distance to the next epoch boundary at the time of writing, which varies between 1 and 720 frames depending on the current frame. See [Epochs and Frames](../../custom/Epochs-And-Frames.md).
