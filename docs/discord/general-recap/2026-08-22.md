# Community Recap — #general

**August 22, 2026** | 12:14 AM UTC – 6:19 AM UTC

## Community Greetings

The day started with a lighthearted "Quil and Chill" weekend vibe from Finn, and a nod to the "big Q3 ahead" from Poorest.

## QNS Name Pricing Discussion

A lengthy conversation emerged around the cost of QNS (Quilibrium Name Service) names. Finn asked how to check which names he owns, and yogurt pointed him to the app UI. Upon checking, Finn was surprised to find that a name he wanted to buy for his daughter now costs 10,000 wQUIL.

blacks1ne noted that 10,000 wQUIL is roughly $60 USD at current prices, which doesn't seem expensive. David agreed, adding that when the token was higher, 10,000 wQUIL was worth $120, and even $250 when wQUIL was at $0.025. Mr.Quil highlighted that the token amount is the real sticker shock: during the early QNS launch, a 20,000 wQUIL name cost about 500–650 wQUIL. That massive discount was a "very nice incentive from Cassie," according to David.

blacks1ne recalled that QNS pre-claim codes had expiry dates, though they were swappable during the validity period. David clarified that unexpired invite codes from the Quorum early access are still valid, as Cassie later confirmed.

## From Cassie (Lead Dev)

Cassie dropped in to say that the team is **revisiting the QNS name pricing**, but that it's "just not the current focus given .25" (likely referring to node version 0.25 or another milestone). She also confirmed that invite codes do not have a hard expiration date set at this time, though they may expire in the future.

David asked if the pricing revision was due to the drop in wQUIL price, but Cassie did not respond to that specific question. The conversation ended with a joking suggestion to create an AI copy of Cassie to work on QNS — a sentiment that was met with laughter.
