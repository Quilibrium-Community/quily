# Community Recap — #general

**November 1, 2026** | 12:02 AM UTC – 11:13 AM UTC

## From Cassie

Cassie shared a write-up on how Binance's hot wallet infrastructure failed, contrasting it with the Bybit incident. She noted that Bybit's cold storage was a Gnosis Safe multisig contract with a few employees holding Ledger wallets for signers — the Safe wallet UI was compromised at the vendor level, with a customer-specific attack payload that other customers didn't notice. In Binance's case, a single DevOps engineer's compromised laptop was involved. Cassie emphasized that an exchange of that size should not be compromised from a single point of intrusion, and noted her experience working on key management infrastructure at Coinbase, assuring that not all exchanges operate this way.

## Privacy-First Design

Tyger explained why Quilibrium can't have something like Etherscan: when you do a transfer, you're essentially transferring the spend key to another address. The details are encrypted — you don't get sender, receiver, token amount, or anything of that nature from the transaction, just that a transaction occurred. Even with the keys of relevant parties, the receiver won't know the sender's address, just that they received a token. Cassie confirmed this was accurate.
