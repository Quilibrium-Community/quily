# Community Recap — #general

**August 30, 2026** | 12:00 AM UTC – 10:54 AM UTC

## From Cassie

Cassie reported that Neynar has closed the bidding process to new entrants, and Quilibrium is one of three finalists under consideration (the other known candidate is Mask Network). She noted that the decision ultimately rests with Neynar, and the community is prepared for either outcome. Separately, she mentioned a new hypersnap-powered client is in development, building on qstorage for media.

On the technical side, Cassie deployed a quasi-fix for the frame sync issue, then followed up with a broader fix and bumped the .25 release. She confirmed no network reset is needed—archives are in the correct state, but regular nodes had not been triggered for the grid reset due to a compatibility code issue with shard key formats. After the update, she observed more joins and seniority merges.

## Network Progress and Fixes

The "freeze" has thawed. Nodes began showing solid 10-second frames with leave requests, and multiple shards were joining and confirming. Mr.Franc reported 7 shards with ~12GB of data, though only 4 had active workers. Cassie explained that as shards split, more workers will be allocated. A merge of shards occurred unexpectedly, but Cassie noted it was a good test—nothing broke, and the upcoming splits should handle similarly.

## Technical Debugging

A detailed analysis by blacks1ne identified a state-jump anchoring bug: fresh nodes request the archive’s current forest head but verify it against the target frame’s pre-state root, causing rejection. Cassie confirmed the issue and is already working on a fix. Store wiping may be needed if nodes show inconsistent prover roots, but after this stage passes, wiping would cause missed proof intervals.
