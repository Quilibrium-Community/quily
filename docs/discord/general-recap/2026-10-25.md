# Community Recap — #general

**October 25, 2026** | 12:16 AM UTC – 10:08 AM UTC

## From Cassie

Cassie addressed concerns about low node rewards, explaining that the current low earnings are expected since the network only hosts the QUIL token's shards. PoMW issuance is based on logical shards getting the most direct coverage, and the QUIL token's shards contain over a million logical shards, resulting in low rewards. However, she noted that most app shards will have a low number of logical shards relative to data, meaning they will earn significantly more once other shards are deployed. She confirmed that compute power will become more valuable relative to the current number of workers.

Cassie also agreed with Black Swan's suggestion to set the seniority succession epoch at the same time as the flag day for PR #899, and confirmed she would cut the 2.1.0.25.102 subpatch once the queued PRs merge.

## Network Updates and PRs

Black Swan reported several technical updates:

- **Seniority succession (PR #959)**: A fix for lost seniority reported in issue #856, allowing old and new keys' seniorities to be combined. It remains inert until `MAINNET_SENIORITY_SUCCESSION_EPOCH` is set, with a suggestion to align it with PR #899's flag day.

- **Archive restart issue**: Restarting all four archives together halted GLOBAL for ~35 minutes because the PeerInfo cache was emptied. A fix (PR #961) keeps the cache across restarts. Until it lands, Black Swan advised restarting archives one at a time.

- **Subpatch 2.1.0.25.102**: Black Swan suggested cutting the subpatch once PRs #962 and #963 merge, which include fixes for archive and regular node issues. PR #930 (delivery-sweep fix) was also queued for inclusion. Release notes were updated.

- **GitHub cleanup**: Black Swan requested closing numerous issues and PRs that were fixed, superseded, or related to removed Go 2.0 code.

## Release Timeline

When asked if the official release is close, Black Swan said "YES," noting they are filing final PRs before suggesting the subpatch bump. Cassie indicated that once .102 is vetted, it would be the right time to set the epoch marker for the two pending PRs and merge them.
