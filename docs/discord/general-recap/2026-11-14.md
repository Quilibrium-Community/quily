# Community Recap — #general

**November 14, 2026** | 12:01 AM UTC – 12:39 PM UTC

## Node Rewards and v2.1.0.25 Questions

A community member asked whether new prover nodes are currently earning QUIL rewards, whether those rewards can be withdrawn and sold, and whether it's better to start now or wait for v2.1.0.25. Tyger clarified that rewards are being earned, but they cannot yet be withdrawn to wQUIL and sold. Starting now was described as an opportunity to begin accumulating good reward rings, though it requires willingness to go through frequent updates.

## Node Performance on .120

Guru_Meditation reported that both of their nodes have been running on .120 since yesterday without issues. They also completed the legacy conversion of their main identity: 18,951 coins shielded, 0 remaining. They noted an early sign of reduced disk writes with .120—one node dropped to ~0.26 TB/day of Quil writes (vs ~0.4–0.6 before). Black Swan confirmed this matches their own observations, explaining that compaction at epoch boundaries now skips stores that barely changed. Cassie responded positively to the report.

## Node Log Warnings

Alternate asked about warnings in node logs, including `app shard consensus stalled` and `storage epoch proof still not delivered`, noting that seniority and rewards are still progressing. They wondered if this is cause for concern or a sign everything is fine.

## From Cassie

Cassie made a brief comment about "Qm Europe, Qn burgertown" and responded "niiiiice" to the node performance report, but made no substantive development announcements today.
