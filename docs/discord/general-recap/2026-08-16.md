# Community Recap — #general

## From Cassie

Cassie addressed several key issues today:

- **Shard state & .25 update**: She noted that reconciling shard state is difficult until the .25 update, which is being finalized. She expected to release the branch update today (Aug 16), after setting up a dry-run test to confirm mainnet state. The update will fix shard splitting and frame progression issues. **No store wipe is needed** for the update.

- **PCDN architecture comparison**: When VELVIL VR asked about Chinese PCDN projects as potential competitors, Cassie explained that those implementations are not open source and not decentralized in control. She noted that breaking those requirements and adding privacy would effectively mean building Quilibrium. She also clarified that the protocol is an outer coordination layer – the execution can be anything, and Quil chose privacy intrinsics.

- **QNS**: Cassie confirmed QNS issues are completely unrelated to the network updates. Transfers are working.

- **Marketing/Forbes**: Regarding a past livestream discussing Forbes relationships, Cassie said the community fund leaders decided not to pursue that further – it wasn't her call.

- **Web3 commitment**: When asked if Quil might retire the token, Cassie stated: "we're not straying from our roots."

## Shard State & Network Performance

Several users reported slow frames and shard state discrepancies. Cassie confirmed slow frames are expected at the moment. VELVIL VR noted a disparity between quilscan.com and app network data; Cassie attributed it to cache/response-timing weirdness.

## PCDN Discussion (neon🦦)

neon🦦 provided extensive context on Chinese PCDN (Peer-to-Peer Content Delivery Network) projects from Alibaba, Xunlei, and others. These systems aggregate low-cost upload bandwidth for commercial companies and use shard-like "slicing." Key points:

- They are not open source, not privacy-focused, and not permissionless – making them fundamentally different from Quil.
- The underlying topology is similar, but Quil adds decentralized control, security, and privacy.
- neon noted these projects are illegal in China for privacy use cases and operate in a different competitive space.

VELVIL VR concluded that Quil is the only "holy grail" of decentralized sharding (security, composability, permissionless participation, scaling). Cassie agreed that the lack of centralized control massively increases difficulty.
