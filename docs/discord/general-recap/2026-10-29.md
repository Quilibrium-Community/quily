## Network Update: 2.1.0.25.111 Rollout

Black Swan provided detailed operator notes for the .111 update. Key points:
- Transfers and shields on shards split from the genesis grid can land again, with session leaders stopping drain and proposing content (#1026)
- Provers without sealed storage earn issuance again from epoch 1321 (GLOBAL frame 951,120, ~02:50Z Oct 29) (#1027)
- Storage labelling works on the leaf due soonest first, yielding to storage work due now (#1025)
- After the bump: wallet scans served to other nodes end one hour after starting, preventing indefinite disk retention

Cassie confirmed all archives have the update. Frame rates returned to 10s baseline and rewards are actively being issued again. Steve reported a healthy ~45 min run on .111 with zero panics, SIGILL, OOM, or restarts, tracking the global head in real time.

## Shield-All Guidance

TawaFiF asked about running shield-all with a legacy identity holding 48,047 coins (553 batches). Black Swan advised:
- Building each batch's proof takes ~5 min on a regular node; archives took 3.5–5.5 min to verify each
- 553 batches ≈ 2 days of back-to-back runs plus a fee per batch
- shield-all has no stop-after-N option, so run one batch first, verify the count drops, then pace the rest

Black Swan later confirmed their resent batch 1 (20 coins) landed end to end on .111, with the plan dropping from 106 to 105 batches. A fix that makes archives verify shields as soon as they arrive is in testing.
