# Community Recap — #general

**November 11, 2026** | 4:27 AM UTC – 12:54 PM UTC

## From Cassie

Cassie released version .25 with .118, a significant update addressing several network issues:

- **Less SSD writing**: Writes only changed replica chunks and preserves completed snapshot leaves across restarts, avoiding redundant encoding
- **Disk-write visibility**: Adds per-epoch write totals and GB/day estimates, broken down by databases, replicas, labels, sectors, and disk activity
- **Fewer consensus stalls**: Moves storage-proof checks off the consensus thread and fixes block eviction that could prevent GLOBAL from recovering
- **More reliable proof delivery**: Bounds queued storage proofs, prioritizes active provers, and drains older proofs first
- **Archive connection reuse**: Shares connections and limits concurrent submissions, reducing connection exhaustion
- **Better worker allocation**: Abandoned seats no longer reserve idle workers; automatic joins keep their intended workers
- **Faster proof verification**: Automatically sizes verifier concurrency using CPU and memory, improves timeout handling, and warns about oversized settings
- **Clearer diagnostics**: Reduces transient health alarms after restarts, explains relabeling and memory waits, and identifies outdated clients
- **Input hardening**: Rejects oversized shard-frame contents before decoding and tightens capacity accounting by sender
- **Scheduled consensus fixes**: Activates silent-prover exclusion at epoch 1490 and credits eligible out-of-order headers exactly once from epoch 1496

Cassie also noted she's investigating ways to streamline node work to avoid overwhelming macOS's memory manager, which is tilted toward consumer behaviors rather than long-running services with high memory demands.

## Network-Wide Shard Deadlock

Black Swan reported that 5 of 110 shards have been deadlocked since 06:41 UTC. At the sealed-storage flag day, GLOBAL never executed the sealing frame, so no snapshot was recorded and no seat on those shards can register. Every leader declines and each new generation stalls the same way. Restarting won't help. A fix is in progress.

## Disk Write Measurements

Black Swan shared measurements from a 15-seat node:
- Epoch 1466: 182 GB written (~1.95 TB/day pace), largest share from worker store compaction plus 32 GB of replica rewrites
- Epoch 1467: 44 GB (~450 GB/day), replica rewrites down to 1.9 GB

Writes scale with seats held. A 220 TBW, 5-year drive budgets about 120 GB/day. From .118, nodes log one line per epoch with `device_gb_per_day` for comparison.

## iOS Vulnerability

Cassie shared a Binance advisory about a vulnerability (worse_query) affecting iOS 26.7.1, 27.0.1, and 27.2 that allows malicious apps to read local files from other apps, putting seed phrases, private keys, and login credentials at risk across the entire ecosystem. This is an iOS-level issue, not unique to Binance. Discussion noted the risk from people installing random TestFlight apps hoping for airdrops.

## Wallet Attack Vectors

Mr.Quil noted increasing attacks on wallets—first Ledger, then Binance, and now the iOS vulnerability—across different attack vectors in a short period.
