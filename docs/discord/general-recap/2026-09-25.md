# Community Recap — #general

**September 25, 2026** | 12:59 AM UTC – 9:34 AM UTC

## From Cassie
Cassie addressed the channel's growing negativity, noting that people have three options: contribute to the project, complain until no one listens, or "touch grass." She encouraged the latter for those who've exhausted option two. On testing progress, she reported the team is "inching closer" — the transactions-over-shard-merge problem is mostly resolved, with specific restart recovery edge cases remaining. She clarified this can't be deferred to version 2.1.1 since it involves regular shard worker behavior, not something archives could sequence around.

## CZ and Mainnet Hopes
A thread kicked off around a CZ-related post, with users debating whether the Binance CEO was referring to Quilibrium. Some noted the account was a parody, though CZ himself had made similar comments days earlier. The consensus leaned toward "we need Quil mainnet" and that CZ was talking about working products, not promises. A link was shared to a BlockChat announcement about being acquired by ZapmeApp and rebranding to ZapChat — a self-custodial, decentralized community platform launching in three months.

## Community Sentiment and Dark Humor
The mood turned self-deprecating as members joked about their losses and doubling down on every release announcement. One user quipped about being "the Sissa of Q" — referencing the legendary inventor of chess — having doubled down after every update to achieve "infinite poverty." Others joked about selling organs and other possessions to front-run the market post-launch. Otter pushed back, suggesting that if people were truly concerned, they should take concrete action like raising funds to invest in Q Inc. or helping with code, noting few actually do.

## Moderation Moment
A user was told to "shut up" and was subsequently muted by Cassie, with the community noting his last 3,000 messages hadn't been chill. The exchange highlighted ongoing friction between frustrated holders and the moderation team.
