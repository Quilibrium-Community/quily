## Node Progress: v2.1.0.25 Update

Cassie confirmed the `.25` update is live and the worker leave issue has been resolved. However, frame intervals began lengthening instead of staying at 10 seconds. She is investigating the root cause of the slowness. Users reported CPU activity returning and nodes recovering after wiping the `store` folder.
