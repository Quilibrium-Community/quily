# Community Recap — #general

**October 15, 2026** | 12:01 AM UTC – 10:32 AM UTC

## From Cassie

Cassie confirmed the `.25` update is live, noting the network halt was caused by prover data volume exceeding execution caps that were "too aggressive" — the caps have been altered and the network should be back to normal shortly.

She also addressed the AI-generated analysis of mainnet blockers, clarifying that the "genesis archive" setup is not a single point of failure since consensus requires 4 of 5 votes. This was an accepted tradeoff for 2.1.0, and 2.1.1 will remove the global archive need entirely. She noted that LLMs tend to be overly pessimistic on timeframes, joking that Claude predicted a two-month timeline for a project it could complete in five minutes.

## Mainnet Status & Node Issues

Mr.Quil shared detailed node error logs showing shard sync failures, archive recovery problems, and unavailable global frames. He noted his node was offline for two days due to local power outages and flooding in Thailand. After returning, his seniority began increasing again.

Black Swan reported that archive submissions still receive buffer-full rejections despite global frame progress, and flagged two GitHub PRs for review:
- [PR #732](https://github.com/QuilibriumNetwork/monorepo/pull/732)
- [PR #733](https://github.com/QuilibriumNetwork/monorepo/pull/733)

Cassie acknowledged the archive issue, saying she'd be updating the archives to resolve it, and that the info revealed "a few other quirks, including why frame stalls causes a kind of stuttering effect."

## Community Analysis of Mainnet Blockers

Mental shared a Claude-generated analysis of the current `.25` codebase identifying three main blockers: the 5-node genesis archive committee, app shard finalization issues, and node catch-up reliability. Mr.Quil pushed back on the timeframe prediction, arguing that identified problems are close to fixed and the network could be ready at any time. Mental agreed the analysis should be taken with a grain of salt.

## Community Sentiment

Several members expressed frustration with repeated timeline promises and the network's performance, while others urged patience and optimism. Subie encouraged the community to focus on building and supporting the network, noting appreciation for Cassie's dedication and Black Swan's contributions. The discussion touched on the project's low market cap relative to its ambitions, with members expressing conviction in the project's long-term potential.
