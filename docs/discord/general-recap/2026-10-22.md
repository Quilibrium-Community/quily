## How App Shard Fixes Work

Finn asked whether app shards were being individually fixed or if bugs were being resolved. Cassie explained it's a combination: identifying who's stuck, finding out why, and fixing what's found so nodes don't get kicked before the next epoch — or they simply get kicked.

Black Swan elaborated: app shards can't be directly fixed because their operation depends on regular nodes' workers proving in and agreeing on valid app frames. If the quorum agrees, frames increment; otherwise, they're stuck. The team identifies disagreement by checking nodes with diagnostic data access enabled and reachable via public IP. Once conditions are clear, they fine-tune agreement terms in sub-patches. The observed issues are no longer due to temporarily centralized components like archive nodes — this is a true decentralized network.
